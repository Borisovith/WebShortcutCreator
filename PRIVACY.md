# Privacy Policy for Web Shortcut Creator

**Last updated:** August 30, 2026

This privacy policy describes how **Web Shortcut Creator** (the "Extension") handles your information.

### 1. Information Collection
**The Extension does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or browsing information.**

- No data is sent to any external server.
- No analytics or tracking tools are used.
- No cookies are created or read.

### 2. Local Storage
The Extension uses Chrome's `storage.sync` (or `storage.local`) solely to store your **user preferences** (such as saving mode and default folder path). This data remains **on your device only** and is never transmitted anywhere.

### 3. Permissions Used
The Extension requests the following permissions, each for a specific purpose:
- `activeTab` Ц to read the URL and title of the currently active tab when you click the extension icon.
- `scripting` Ц to extract the website's favicon from the active page.
- `nativeMessaging` Ц to communicate with the local native host that creates the shortcut file on your computer.
- `storage` Ц to save your settings (save mode and default folder path).

All actions are performed locally on your machine. No data leaves your device.

### 4. Third-Party Access
The Extension does not use any third-party services, APIs, or libraries that collect user data.
